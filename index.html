MacroSignal

In progress · Python, LangChain, LLaMA 3 (Groq), FAISS, XGBoost, FastAPI

A geopolitical market signal engine. It ingests news, pulls out entities and sentiment with LLaMA 3, and feeds those features to an XGBoost model that produces directional signals across 24 financial assets. Validated chronologically to avoid look-ahead bias, it currently sits at 75–79% directional accuracy. A FAISS index over 26,000 news passages backs the retrieval side, and prompt and retrieval tuning raised evidence-grounded answer accuracy from 72% to 86% on 180 annotated queries.
